      Worth knowing

      More than four in five new Australian home loans are now arranged by a broker.

      One person, not five banks

      Rather than approaching several lenders yourself, you work with one broker who tracks the detail across all of them.

      A panel of lenders

      We compare products across a panel of lenders, not just one bank’s own range, and explain the trade-offs in plain language.

      A legal duty to you

      Since 1 January 2021, Australian mortgage brokers have been required by law to act in the best interests of the borrower.

      Before you get in touch

      Does it cost anything to speak with you?

      An initial conversation is free. If we proceed, how we’re paid is explained up front in our Credit Guide.

      How much deposit do I need?

      It varies by loan type and lender. Eligible first home buyers can buy with a 5% deposit under the Australian Government 5% Deposit Scheme. A deposit under 20% outside a scheme usually means paying lenders mortgage insurance. We can talk through what applies to you.

      How long does an enquiry take?

      The first conversation usually takes 15 to 20 minutes. From there, timelines depend on your situation and the lender.

      Is this a loan offer?

      No. Everything on this site is general information, not a recommendation or a confirmation that you are eligible. Rates, fees and eligibility are set by individual lenders and depend on your circumstances.
